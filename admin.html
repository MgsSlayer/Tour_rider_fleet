Base locations

Bases your vehicles depart from. Repositioning is measured from whichever base is the shortest drive to the pickup.

How repositioning works. When a pickup is more than 40 miles or 1 hour from the nearest base, a $150 fee covers the empty drive to reach the customer. That fee is added into the base fare rather than shown as its own line, so the customer sees a single base fare, never a surcharge. The suggestion panel always tells you which base was used and how much is included.
